# Scope interpretation and remaining acceptance

The governing documents are the user's two original pasted briefs, plus later requests for mobile liquid-glass presentation, local AI first, subscription-backed Astra, desktop launch, automatic catalog refresh, volcano activity, GitHub delivery and Blocpod copyright. This audit does not replace their scope.

## Requirements versus suggestions

- Brief 2 section 27 calls Unreal the **preferred** engine and lists complementary technologies as **potential**. The required result is the specified Earth presentation and interaction quality. Rewriting the working renderer in Unreal is not independently required.
- Section 31 calls the six field displays **possible render modes**. Scientific presentation and the ability to disable cinematic effects are explicit requirements. Implementing every suggested field mode is not an independent completion gate.
- Section 40 is a **suggested technical stack**. Existing local implementations must satisfy behavior, provenance and reliability; particular database or stream brands are not deliverables by themselves.
- Sections 20, 21, 42 and 52 require honest scoring, comparison, counterevidence and frozen records. Section 52 explicitly says the mission is not to prove the methodology correct. Established earthquake-prediction skill, a favorable model ranking or statistical significance are not conditions for software completion. Unsupported probabilities must remain unavailable; negative or pending evidence must remain visible.
- The user's later direction explicitly permits an illustrative configurable route graph pending canonical source encoding. Do not manufacture canonical corridors. Their later direction also excluded a packaged installer; the desktop shortcut and reliable launcher remain required.
- Physical mobile/XR acceptance and subjective visual superiority remain unverified. Browser viewport results cannot substitute for those checks.

## Concrete presentation gap closed in this checkpoint

Section 30 explicitly requests a depth ruler in the deep-event inspection experience. The prior inspector showed a depth number and surface-to-hypocenter line, without a ruler. It now includes a linear kilometer ruler based on catalog depth, independent of the globe's exaggeration setting. Actual Fiji event us6000ty1a renders at 602.2 km in the mobile inspector. Numerical checks cover shallow, deep, negative and greater-than-700 km depths. Evidence: `artifacts/depth-ruler-phone.png`.

## Remaining deep-event experience

The existing selection enters X-ray, focuses the event, retains source metadata and exposes model-path inspection. Selecting a deep event now loads published Slab2 contours and lists the current analysis candidates that cite that source, with direct links to their evidence/counterevidence. The real Fiji event exposed two related watches at phone width and the first opened correctly. Stale asynchronous selection/cutoff changes cannot enable contours for an obsolete request. The view labels Slab2 as static reference geometry, not a confirmed association. Slab contours now fade in over 700 ms during deep selection, with immediate display in scientific or reduced-motion modes. The camera eases toward the event while a white inspection cursor descends to the rendered hypocenter, without moving catalog observations. Selecting a deep source now highlights its source-linked paths and targets within the active watch filters and layer visibility. Endpoint rendering and switching were checked in the running phone viewport; subsecond motion is covered by geometry tests, while perceptual animation acceptance, in-headset acceptance and physical-phone acceptance remain open.

The broader implemented-feature and evidence inventory remains in BUILD-STATUS.md. Its older chronological entries describe checkpoints, not current unresolved gates; later entries can supersede their missing-feature notes. The full goal remains active.

## Swarm measurements (§14)

The earlier engine returned bin count, maximum magnitude and approximate moment only. It now also computes exact pairwise spherical diameter for the retained 20 bins, early-to-late centroid distance/bearing and mean depth difference, and equal-duration-half event rates with signed change. Research exposes values, source IDs and methods. Missing temporal contrast remains unavailable. These are summaries of the existing two-degree bins, not a fitted swarm detector; source moments still assume Mw-equivalence across native magnitude scales. Existing redistribution routing and forecast scores are unchanged. Broader claims about target splitting, causal accumulation, automatic global concentric discovery and full graph-topology analogue matching are not proved by these summaries and remain separate audit items.
